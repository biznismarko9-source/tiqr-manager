TIQR Manager - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 August 2026

TIQR Manager is a local-first desktop application for tracking purchased tickets, orders and sales. It has no server of its own: everything you enter is stored only in a database file on your own computer. This page explains the one exception - the optional Google Sheets integration under Settings → Integrations - and exactly what it does with your Google data.

What the Google Sheets integration is for

The integration lets you connect one Google Sheet to TIQR Manager's "Pulls" data, so rows you type into that sheet can be read into the app, and (for a sheet the app creates for you) written back out. It has two independent ways to connect:

  • Sign in with Google. You sign in with your own Google account through Google's own sign-in page in your browser - TIQR Manager never sees your Google password. The app requests exactly two permissions: read/write access to Google Sheets (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets), and your email address, so the app can show you which account is signed in (openid, email). It does not request access to Drive, Gmail, Calendar, Contacts, or anything else in your Google account.
  • A shared connection, without signing in. As an alternative, the app can use one fixed Google "service account" (a machine identity, not a personal Google account) that the app's developer controls. You either share an existing sheet with that account's email address, or let the app create a new sheet and share it with an email address you type in. This path never asks for or sees your Google password either.

What data is accessed, and what happens to it

Whichever way you connect, the only Google data TIQR Manager ever reads or writes is the content of the one spreadsheet you connected - the rows and columns you can see in that sheet yourself. When you sign in with Google, the app also reads the email address of the signed-in account, once, to show "Signed in as ...". Nothing else in your Google account is read, and nothing is shared with any third party. All communication happens directly between your computer and Google's own servers over an encrypted connection; TIQR Manager has no server in between that your data passes through or is copied to.

What is stored, and where

If you sign in with Google, a long-lived sign-in token and your email address are stored locally, in the same database file on your computer where the rest of your TIQR Manager data already lives. They are never uploaded anywhere. Clicking "Sign out" in the app deletes them immediately. Which spreadsheet is connected (its ID and tab name) is stored the same way, locally, and is deleted immediately when you click "Disconnect".

Revoking access

Besides signing out inside TIQR Manager, you can revoke its access at any time directly from your Google Account, under Security → Third-party apps & services. Doing so immediately invalidates the sign-in token TIQR Manager was using; the app will simply ask you to sign in again the next time you use that connection.

Changes to this policy

If what the app accesses or how it handles that data ever changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change accordingly.
